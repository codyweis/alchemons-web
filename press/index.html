About the game

Short: Alchemons is a creature-alchemy RPG for iOS and Android. Fuse and extract 137 species, descend into seventeen guarded worlds, and find out who made the valley beautiful, and why.

You wake as an alchemist in a beautiful valley you don't remember, with a laboratory and a single vial. Fuse two Alchemons and extract what they make: each vial inherits its parents' nature, potential, size and tint. Harvest wild Alchemons from painted wilderness scenes, and fuse the ones you find there.

Then you find a ship, and the valley stops being the whole world. Past it is open space, and every one of its seventeen planets has a guardian that protects it from alchemy. Each planet's dungeon is a puzzle built on its element's physics: route fire through a cathedral, steer a storm circuit, read the tides in a mirrored temple. Earn the stars, overcome the guardian, and take its relic to an altar to summon a Mystic.

Alongside the campaign: endless Survival with a mastery tree for each of the eight families, contests in beauty, speed, strength and intelligence, raids, a 50-level alchemy puzzle mode, and a home biome your Alchemons actually live in.

Key features

  • 137 species in eight families (Let, Pip, Mane, Horn, Mask, Wing, Kin, Mystic), each in every element
  • Living genetics: 27 natures, four potentials rated 1–100, and inherited size and tint genes
  • Seventeen planet dungeons, each a hand-built puzzle around one element's rules, with three stars to earn
  • A story told through the world: curiosity, unease and the question of what collecting costs
  • Survival waves with eight family mastery trees, plus contests, raids and alchemy puzzles
  • Plays fully offline, with optional cloud saves
