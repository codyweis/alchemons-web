Fusion Codex

Two elements go in. One comes out.

Every Alchemon is made of its element. Fuse two and the grains decide what they become. Pick two to try a formula from the game's codex.

Pick an element

The Lets

Small, bright, and made by hand.

Every family of Alchemons takes a form in every element. The Lets come first: seventeen of them, one for each element. Each one you extract inherits its parents' nature, potential, size and tint.

    • 27natures that change how an Alchemon harvests, breeds and fights
    • 4potentials rated 1–100: speed, strength, intelligence and beauty
    • 11size and tint genes, from tiny to giant, thermal to albino
    • 8families, each with its own mastery tree

    Alchemical Biomes

    Wild places, painted in grains.

    Each biome keeps the hour on your phone's clock, with the real phase of the moon, its own weather, and wild Alchemons that won't be there again once you leave.

      Everything you make, you use

      One collection. Every way to play.

      The wilderness

      Harvest wild Alchemons from the biomes by day and night, or fuse the two standing in front of you.

      Open space

      Fly a party of three across the star chart: planets, stations, raids and wild encounters.

      Planet dungeons

      Puzzle dungeons built on each element's physics: route fire, steer storms, flood the fen.

      Survival

      Hold the core against endless waves. Pick each family's mastery path before the run locks it in.

      Alchemy puzzles

      Fifty drag-and-drop levels on the rules of transmutation. Fewer moves, more stars.

      A home of your own

      Arrange a home biome for your residents: scenery, weather, keepsakes and decor. They'll find things to do with all of it.

      Contests

      Four arenas across the cosmos test what you bred for. Master one and it's crowned in gold.
