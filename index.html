    Breeding

    A living thing leaves the vial. You call it a discovery.The word comes easily.

    Every Alchemon has four Potentials, from 1 to 100: Speed, Strength, Intelligence and Beauty. A child takes each one from one parent or the other, or rolls a new value.

    Two of each Alchemon's stats are Dominant and pass down more reliably. Fusing within a family only reshuffles what the parents have. Crossing two families with the right recipe is the only way a line climbs past both of them.

    The perfect Alchemon isn't found. It's bred, one generation at a time.

    • 4Potentials, from 1 to 100: Speed, Strength, Intelligence and Beauty
    • 2Dominant stats in every Alchemon, passed down more reliably
    • 27natures that change how an Alchemon harvests, breeds and fights
    • 11size and tint genes, from tiny to giant, thermal to albino
    • 2known mutations from fusing in the wild, Alchemized and Transmuted. There may be more.

      Game modes

      You follow the ship beyond the world that soothed you.Something there remembers your work.

      The Alchemons you breed are the ones you take into the wild, into open space and down into its planets.

      The wild

      Harvest wild Alchemons by day and night, or fuse with the ones you find there.

      Open space

      Find the ship and fly a party across the star chart to planets, stations and raids.

      Planet dungeons

      Seventeen planets, each a puzzle dungeon built on its element's rules, with a guardian at the end.

      Survival

      Hold the core against endless waves, with a mastery tree for each family.

      Alchemy puzzles

      45 levels built on the fusion rules. Solve each in fewer moves for more stars.

      A home realm

      Choose a realm to live in and furnish it with scenery, weather, keepsakes and decor. Each realm keeps its own residents.

      Relics and Mystics

      Beat a planet's guardian, bring its relic to the altar, and summon a Mystic.
