Fusion

Breed new Alchemons by fusing elements

Every Alchemon belongs to one of seventeen elements. Fuse two and you get a new Alchemon, usually of a third element, with odds set by the pairing. Pick two elements below to see a recipe from the game.

The Lets

Seventeen Lets, one for each element

There are eight families of Alchemons, and each family has a form in every element. The Lets are the first family you meet. Every Alchemon you extract inherits its nature, potential, size and tint from its parents.

    • 27natures that change how an Alchemon harvests, breeds and fights
    • 4potentials rated 1–100: speed, strength, intelligence and beauty
    • 11size and tint genes, from tiny to giant, thermal to albino
    • 8families, each with its own mastery tree
    • 2known mutations from fusing in the wild, Alchemized and Transmuted. There may be more.

    Wild realms

    Eight realms to harvest from

    Each realm runs on your phone's clock and the real phase of the moon, with its own weather. The wild Alchemons you find there are gone once you leave. Six are shown here; the Glass Dunes and Geode Hollow are in the game too.

      Game modes

      One collection for every mode

      The Alchemons you breed are the ones you take into the wild, into space, into dungeons and into contests.

      The wild

      Harvest wild Alchemons by day and night, or fuse with the ones you find there.

      Open space

      Find the ship and fly a party across the star chart to planets, stations and raids.

      Planet dungeons

      Seventeen planets, each a puzzle dungeon built on its element's rules, with a guardian at the end.

      Survival

      Hold the core against endless waves, with a mastery tree for each family.

      Alchemy puzzles

      45 levels built on the fusion rules. Solve each in fewer moves for more stars.

      A home realm

      Choose a realm to live in and furnish it with scenery, weather, keepsakes and decor. Each realm keeps its own residents.

      Relics and Mystics

      Beat a planet's guardian, bring its relic to the altar, and summon a Mystic.

      Contests

      Four arenas each judge one thing you bred for. The fifth, the Celestial Nexus, judges all four at once.
