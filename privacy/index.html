Privacy policy

Effective October 8, 2026. This policy covers the Alchemons app for iOS and Android and this website.

The short version: Alchemons plays offline, and your game lives on your device. We only collect information when you choose to create an account, make a purchase or join the waitlist. We don't show ads, don't use advertising or analytics trackers, and don't sell your information.

Information on your device

Your collection, progress and settings are stored locally on your device. If you allow notifications, reminders (for example, when something you started has finished) are scheduled on your device; no server sends them. If the game uses your device's motion sensors for visual effects, that data stays on the device and is never recorded or sent anywhere.

If you create an account

An account is optional. It enables cloud saves and moving your game to a new device. If you create one, we store:

  • your email address, display name and a securely hashed password (handled by Google Firebase Authentication);
  • a copy of your save data, so it can be restored on another device;
  • an identifier for the device that currently holds your active session, and records of device transfers you start.

We use this information only to run your account, sync your save and secure transfers.

If you make a purchase

Purchases are processed by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment card details. To deliver what you bought and prevent fraud, our server verifies each purchase receipt and records its transaction identifier, the item and your resulting entitlement.

If you join the waitlist

We store the email address you enter, the platform you pick and when you signed up. We use it for one purpose: to tell you when Alchemons is available. Your entry is stored under a one-way fingerprint of your email so a repeat signup doesn't create duplicates.

The website

This site has no cookies, analytics or tracking scripts. It is served by Google Firebase Hosting and loads fonts from Google Fonts. Like any web server, they may briefly process your IP address and browser details to deliver the page. See Google's privacy policy.

Who processes your information

We use Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions and Hosting) to store and process the information above, and Apple and Google to process purchases. We don't sell or rent personal information or share it for advertising. We would only disclose it if required by law.

How long we keep it

Account and save data are kept while your account exists. Purchase records are kept as long as needed to honor your purchases and meet legal obligations. Waitlist entries are deleted once launch announcements are done, or sooner if you ask.

Your choices and rights

You can play without an account. You can ask us to access, correct, export or delete your account, save data or waitlist entry by emailing hello@alchemons.com. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have additional rights, and we will honor them.

Children

Alchemons is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. Children can play offline without creating an account. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access rules restrict account data to its owner, and purchase records are accessible only to our servers.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the effective date above. For significant changes, we'll also tell you in the app.
